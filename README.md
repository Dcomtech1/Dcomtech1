## 🚀 About Me

I'm a **Data Scientist and Machine Learning Engineer** with hands-on experience across the full ML lifecycle — from statistical modelling and feature engineering to LLM benchmarking and automated evaluation pipelines.

Currently working in **AI evaluation at Abundant AI**, where I design and validate data science workflows across Python, R, Julia, and Octave, and build quality-assurance systems that test the reliability and interpretability of ML outputs. I've also evaluated AI models professionally for SME Careers, assessing reasoning quality, hallucinations, and edge-case failures at scale.

I hold a **B.Tech in Computer Science** (CGPA: 4.35/5.0) from LAUTECH and an **AWS Future AI Scientist Nanodegree**. I care deeply about building models that are not just accurate, but explainable and trustworthy.
